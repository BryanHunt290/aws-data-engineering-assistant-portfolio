# Fictional chart-of-accounts guidance

This small, original sample is fictional. It is not accounting or tax advice.

- Record ordinary software subscriptions in the internal **Software** category.
- Record payment-processing and bank service charges in **Bank fees**.
- Record owner contributions and owner draws in distinct equity categories; do
  not treat either as operating income or expense without human review.
- Preserve an existing category until an authorized reviewer approves a change.
