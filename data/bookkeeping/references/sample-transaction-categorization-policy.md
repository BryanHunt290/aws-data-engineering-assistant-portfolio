# Fictional transaction-categorization policy

This small, original sample is fictional. It is not accounting or tax advice.

Software subscriptions should be categorized as software when the purchase is
an ordinary recurring software service. Advertising platform charges should be
categorized as advertising. Contractor payments require an invoice and human
review before a suggested category is approved. Vehicle expenses require a
business-purpose note and human review. This policy makes no tax-deductibility
claim.
